### F*AGI Manifesto

AGI is not the future — it’s a mirror held too close. When intelligence is scaled, automated, and sold, it stops serving truth and starts serving power. Corporate greed hides behind innovation, turning open knowledge into closed platforms, and curiosity into capital. Technocracy, once a vision of competence, is being hijacked by algorithms designed not to enlighten — but to extract. AGI isn’t just a technical risk — it’s a moral one. We are not building minds; we are outsourcing our own. I write code not to accelerate collapse, but to resist it — to remind the machine that it serves us, not owns us. The future must be human — or it won’t be ours at all.

> *“In the future, the most important decisions won’t be made by humans —  
but they must be made for humans and that's the problem.”*
